# PROJECT: HIREFLOW AI — SMART RECRUITMENT AUTOMATION PLATFORM BY (Fixr)

Act as a senior full-stack developer, UI/UX designer, software architect, database engineer, and AI application developer.

Build a modern, responsive, production-oriented full-stack recruitment automation web application called **HireFlow AI**.

Tagline: "Find talent. Connect smarter. Hire faster."

The application should help HR executives and recruiters organize candidate information, discover suitable candidates from authorized data sources, create recruitment campaigns, send approved personalized emails, collect voluntary expressions of interest, and manage interested candidates through a centralized dashboard.

This must be a functional full-stack application, not merely a static UI or a frontend prototype.

## 1. CORE OBJECTIVE

Develop a recruitment workflow that reduces repetitive administrative work.

The primary workflow is:

1. HR executive signs in.
2. HR executive creates a job opportunity.
3. The system identifies suitable candidates from authorized candidate records.
4. The recruiter reviews and approves the candidate list.
5. The recruiter creates and previews a personalized recruitment email campaign.
6. The system sends approved emails through a configured email provider.
7. Candidates can voluntarily express interest using a secure response page.
8. Candidates confirm their contact details and submit the required information.
9. The backend stores their responses.
10. Interested candidates automatically appear in the appropriate HR dashboard section.
11. The recruiter reviews, contacts, and updates each candidate's status.

All sensitive operations must happen on the backend.

## 12. CANDIDATE INTEREST PAGE

The response page should display the relevant job details and recruiter identity.

- "I'm Interested"
- "Not Interested"

When the candidate selects "I'm Interested", show a form requesting only the information needed for the recruitment process.

Suggested fields:

- Name.
- Verified email address.
- Mobile number, optional unless necessary for the specific next step.
- Preferred location.
- Relevant experience.
- CV upload, if required.
- Preferred contact time.
- Optional message.

Ask the candidate to confirm that their submitted information is accurate.

Display a clear privacy notice explaining why the information is collected, who will receive it, and how the candidate can exercise applicable privacy rights.

Use a separate, optional and unbundled preference for future recruitment communications where required.

The initial response must not automatically enroll candidates in unrelated marketing campaigns.

Secure response links with cryptographically strong random tokens, limited scope, expiration where appropriate, and server-side validation.

Do not expose personal information in URL query parameters.

Protect against token replay, duplicate submissions, unauthorized updates, and automated form abuse.

Provide a confirmation page after submission.

Example confirmation:

"Thank you for your response. Your interest has been recorded. An authorized recruiter may contact you regarding this opportunity."

When the candidate selects "Not Interested", update their response status appropriately and do not continue the same recruitment outreach.

Provide a way to report an incorrect or unwanted message.
